# Slide Outline — Web3Geeks Client Inquiry Qualification Agent
## 5-7 Minute Presentation

---

### Slide 1: Problem (1 minute)
**Title:** Manual Client Inquiry Processing is Slow and Inconsistent

**Key Points:**
- Web3Geeks receives 20+ inquiries daily
- Manual review takes 15-30 minutes per inquiry
- Different team members qualify leads differently
- Cannot scale without hiring more staff

**Visual:** Show current workflow (manual process)

---

### Slide 2: Solution (1 minute)
**Title:** Automated Qualification Agent

**Key Points:**
- AI-powered agent that qualifies inquiries automatically
- Validates input, researches companies, scores leads
- Drafts personalized responses
- Human approves before sending

**Visual:** Show new workflow (automated process)

---

### Slide 3: Architecture (1 minute)
**Title:** How It Works

**Key Points:**
- 5 nodes: Validate -> Research -> Qualify -> Draft -> Human Approve
- LangGraph for control and state management
- External tools: Company database, LLM
- Human-in-the-loop at critical decisions

**Visual:** Architecture diagram
