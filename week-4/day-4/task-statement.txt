Week 4 — Day 4: Workflows, Scheduling & Business Automation

📌 Scenario: The voice agent now becomes a business assistant.

Task 1 — Google Calendar Integration
Create calendar events. Include:
- Client name
- Phone
- Employee
- Property
- Date
- Time
- Meeting notes

Task 2 — Email Automation
Automatically email the assigned employee. Include:
- Meeting time
- Property
- Client details
- Requirements

Task 3 — Appointment Management
Support:
- Booking
- Rescheduling
- Cancellation
Update both Calendar and email notifications.

Task 4 — Workflow Automation
Build an n8n workflow: Call → Intent → Property Match → Appointment → Calendar → Email → CRM Update
Handle failures and retries.

Task 5 — CRM Logging
Store:
- Call transcripts
- Client preferences
- Appointment history
- Follow-up reminders
