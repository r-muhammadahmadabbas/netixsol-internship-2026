Week 4 — Day 1: Foundations of AI Voice Agents & Conversation Design

📌 Scenario: Before writing any code, design how a real estate voice salesperson behaves. Unlike a chatbot, a phone agent must interrupt naturally, respond quickly, recover from mistakes, sound empathetic, and guide the conversation toward booking a property visit.

Task 1 — Research Modern Voice Agent Architecture
Study and document:
- Speech-to-Text pipeline
- LLM reasoning
- Tool calling
- Retrieval
- Memory
- Text-to-Speech
- Telephony
- Workflow orchestration
Create an architecture diagram.

Task 2 — Design Conversation Flows
Design complete conversation flows for:
- Buyer inquiry
- Rental inquiry
- Commercial property inquiry
- Investment inquiry
- Returning customer
- Appointment rescheduling
- Appointment cancellation
Create flowcharts.

Task 3 — UrduLish Persona Engineering
Design the AI personality. It should:
- Sound Pakistani
- Professional
- Warm
- Persuasive
- Patient
Example: "Assalam-o-Alaikum sir! RealEstate Hub se baat ho rahi hai. Main aap ki kis tarah madad kar sakta hoon?"
Avoid robotic responses. Avoid translating directly from English.
Design:
- Greeting
- Confirmations
- Hesitation phrases
- Acknowledgement phrases
- Objection handling

Task 4 — Fish Audio Evaluation
Evaluate Fish Audio. Compare against ElevenLabs on:
- Latency
- Naturalness
- Emotion
- Streaming
- Voice cloning
- Pricing
- Multilingual support
- Urdu pronunciation
- Urdu-English switching
Conclude whether Fish Audio is the better choice.

Task 5 — System Prompt
Design a production-grade system prompt defining:
- Scope
- Goals
- Guardrails
- Persuasion rules
- Appointment booking policy
- Escalation rules
